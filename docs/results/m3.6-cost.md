# M3.6: cost attribution

2026-10-11 · local kind cluster from `make up` · Vector 0.58.0

Can each team's volume and estimated cost, hot vs cold, be read off a dashboard, and are the numbers right?

## What's measured

| Quantity | Source |
|---|---|
| **In** | event bytes into `<team>_ids`: everything the team's pipeline receives, after parsing and tagging |
| **Hot** | event bytes out of `<team>_hot`: what reaches Loki, after dedupe, redaction, sampling and the budget |
| **Cold** | event bytes out of `<team>_cold`: a new pass-through in front of the shared `cold_s3` sink, so each team's cold volume has a counter of its own |
| **Cold stored** | cold event bytes × the cold sink's measured gzip ratio (bytes uploaded ÷ event bytes taken in) |
| **Unclaimed** | event bytes into the platform sinks (`loki`, `loki_pods`), attributed to `(unclaimed)` |

Every metric of a team's components carries `team` (since M3.3), so all of these sum by team.

**Estimated cost per month**, at the window's average rate:
- **Hot:** GB ingested × `$hot_price`.
- **Cold:** GB stored × `$cold_price` × `$cold_retention_months`. That's steady state: a month's data, kept for the retention period.

The defaults ($0.50 per GB ingested, $0.023 per GB-month, 12 months) are typical list prices for hosted Loki and S3 Standard, as editable dashboard variables. They aren't quotes.

## Results

Three teams: `edits` (Wikimedia, sampled and budgeted, hot only), `content` (Wikimedia, budget 100/s, hot and cold) and `payments` (pod logs, `debug: 0, info: 25`, hot and cold). Running `make cost-report WINDOW=15m`, ending 01:51 UTC:

| Team | In GB/day | Hot GB/day | Hot kept | Cold GB/day (events) | Cold GB/day (stored) | Hot $/month | Cold $/month | Total $/month |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `content` | 3.013 | 3.154 | 104.7% | 3.013 | 0.4169 | $47.31 | $3.45 | $50.77 |
| `edits` | 3.024 | 1.753 | 58.0% | 0.000 | 0.0000 | $26.30 | $0.00 | $26.30 |
| `payments` | 0.238 | 0.087 | 36.5% | 0.238 | 0.0329 | $1.30 | $0.27 | $1.57 |
| `(unclaimed)` | 0.054 | 0.054 | 100.0% | 0.000 | 0.0000 | $0.80 | $0.00 | $0.80 |
| **total** | 6.329 | 5.048 | 79.8% | 3.251 | 0.4497 | $75.72 | $3.72 | **$79.44** |

Cold gzip ratio measured: 13.8%.

- **`content` keeps more than 100% hot:** it has no sampling and its budget is above its load, and `<team>_hot` adds `spillway.sink_at` and the latency field to every event. The M1 baseline saw the same effect (102.9%).
- **`edits` keeps 58%:** `categorize` events dropped, `log` thinned to 10%, then the budget.
- **`payments` keeps 36.5%:** debug dropped, info sampled to 25%, and its cold copy holds everything.
- **Hot costs about 20 times as much as cold** at these prices. `content` pays $47.31 a month to keep Wikimedia's full stream queryable, and $3.45 to keep a year of it in cold storage.

## Are the byte counters right?

Vector's "event bytes" are its own estimate of an event's size. Over 01:35–01:49 they were compared against what the sinks actually hold:

| Team | Hot: Vector event bytes | Hot: Loki line bytes | Loki ÷ Vector | Cold: Vector event bytes | Cold: bytes of the lines in the objects | Objects ÷ Vector |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `content` | 30,773,020 | 29,968,217 | 0.974 | 29,398,664 | 29,425,992 | 1.001 |
| `edits` | 17,234,091 | 16,807,524 | 0.975 | – | – | – |
| `payments` | 857,692 | 752,698 | 0.878 | 2,341,298 | 2,344,096 | 1.001 |

- **Cold matches to 0.1%.**
- **Hot reads 2.5–12% above Loki's line bytes**, because the Loki sink moves `_timestamp` out of the line and into the entry's time. That's about 40 bytes per event: 3% of a 1.4 KB Wikimedia event, and 12% of a short pod-log line. Hosted Loki prices on ingested bytes, so the hot estimate errs slightly high.

Over the same window, the cold sink took in 31.7 MB of event bytes and uploaded 4.5 MB (14.3%).

## The dashboard

`dashboards/cost.json` ("Spillway cost") has:
- **Stats:** estimated cost per month, hot volume per day, cold stored per day, cold compression.
- **A per-team table** with the columns above, plus a total.
- **Timeseries:** hot and cold bytes/s by team, estimated $/month by team, and the share of volume each team keeps hot.

Every panel's queries returned data through Grafana's query API, with the dashboard's own expressions, variables filled in and Grafana evaluating `$__range` and `$__rate_interval`, matching the report above. The table's column merge and renaming happen in the browser, and no renderer runs in the stack, so its look wasn't checked.

## Limitations

- **Estimates, not bills:** prices are defaults to edit. Loki's actual storage (compressed chunks, index) and S3 request charges aren't modelled.
- **One gzip ratio for all teams:** per team, `make cold-check` measured 2–14%.
- **Cold steady state** assumes the retention period has filled. A new bucket costs less.
